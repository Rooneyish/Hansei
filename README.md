# Hansei: Your AI Clinical Companion

**Hansei (反省)** is a digital well-being application designed to help you practice the Japanese philosophy of self-reflection. It acts as a supportive "clinical companion," helping you navigate your daily emotions, reframe negative thoughts, and maintain a consistent mindfulness routine.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 📖 Intelligent Journaling
Log your daily thoughts and feelings easily. You can type them out or use the **Scan** feature to turn your handwritten notes into digital entries instantly.

### 🧠 Emotion Insights
As you journal, our smart system identifies your emotional state. It helps you visualize your progress through the **Hansei Mirror**, showing your mood trends and emotional balance over the week.

### 🧪 The CBT Lab
Got a negative or stressful thought? The CBT Lab helps you break it down and provides a helpful "reframe" to help you look at the situation from a more positive and balanced perspective.

### 🧘 The Zen Room
A dedicated space for mindfulness. Set a **Zen Intention** based on how you feel right now, and use our meditation timer and calming background sounds to find your center.

### 🎵 Mindful Music
Receive personalized music recommendations that match your detected mood to help you relax, focus, or lift your spirits.

### 🛡️ Safety First
Your well-being is our priority. If the system detects signs of a clinical crisis, it will immediately provide localized helpline numbers and professional resources to ensure you get the support you need.

---

## 🎮 Rewards & Habits
*   **Gold Lacquer Rewards:** Earn points for completing your daily reflections, inspired by the art of *Kintsugi* (the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold).
*   **Quests:** Complete well-being challenges to progress through different stages of your self-improvement journey.
*   **Streaks:** Build a lasting habit by keeping your daily reflection streak alive.

---

> **Note:** Hansei is a tool designed for self-reflection and general well-being. It is not a replacement for professional therapy, medical advice, or psychiatric care.
